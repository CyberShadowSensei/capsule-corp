--- COMPLAINT DESCRIPTION ---

We have received reports from three different hospital pharmacists within our network regarding unusual tablet appearance and reported adverse events from patients consuming tablets from the above batch.

Complaint Type: Product Quality Defect with suspected Adverse Drug Reaction

Detailed Account:
Pharmacist at City General Hospital (Dr. Priya Mehta) flagged that tablets from pack lot ATV24907B-P3 were visibly discolored - presenting with a yellowish hue instead of the standard white. A subsequent complaint from Regional Medical Center noted that two patients (Patient IDs anonymized as P-001 and P-002) reported nausea and unusual fatigue within 48 hours of switching to this batch. A third report from Lakeside Clinic described tablets as having a slightly unusual odor.

The complaints span a period from 2026-07-18 to 2026-07-21. All three facilities have quarantined their remaining stock from this batch.

Additionally, our internal dissolution testing on 6 retained samples showed dissolution rates below specification in 2 samples (67% and 71% vs the 80% minimum at 30 minutes Q value). This is potentially indicative of a formulation or manufacturing process deviation.

--- REGULATORY CONTEXT ---

Given the suspected adverse drug reactions and out-of-spec dissolution results, we are treating this as a potentially reportable event under Schedule Y of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act. We are filing this complaint concurrently with our local pharmacovigilance authority.

We request:
1. Immediate batch recall assessment
2. A full investigation report within 15 business days
3. Root cause analysis with CAPA documentation
4. Regulatory notification support

This complaint is being escalated directly to your Quality Director and Regulatory Affairs team.
